Relay

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Relay Discord bot (“Relay”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect only what is needed to make the Bot work.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features a server uses, Relay may process and store:

  • Discord identifiers: server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and user IDs, needed to apply settings and to record moderation actions.
  • Server settings: configuration you choose, such as anti-spam and duplicate-filter thresholds, the log channel, and exempt roles or channels.
  • Moderation records: where logs are enabled, details of actions taken (for example the user ID, the type of action, the reason and the time).
  • Server template data: when you save a template, the structure you asked us to save, such as role names, colors and permissions, channel and category names, order and permission overwrites, and basic server settings.
  • Diagnostic information: command usage and error details (such as timestamps and IDs) used to fix bugs and keep the Bot running.

3. Message content

To detect spam and repeated messages, Relay may read messages in servers where those features are on. Message content is used in real time and, where needed, kept only briefly in memory to compare recent messages. It is not saved to our database and is not used for advertising or to train any model.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect passwords, payment information, email addresses, or your private direct messages. Relay does not save messages or member lists as part of a server template.

5. How we use information

  • to provide and run Relay’s features in your server;
  • to show moderation logs to the staff who configured them;
  • to save and load the templates you request;
  • to prevent abuse, fix bugs and improve the Bot;
  • to meet legal obligations or enforce our Terms.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it for advertising. Data may be handled by the hosting provider that runs the Bot, and we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users. Discord processes data under its own Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep server data only for as long as it is needed to provide the features. When Relay is removed from a server, that server’s stored data is deleted within 30 days. Server owners and administrators can ask us to delete their server’s data, including saved templates and logs, at any time, and users can ask us to delete or correct records that relate to their user ID, by contacting us below.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we hold, such as keeping the Bot’s credentials private and limiting who can access the stored data. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Relay is intended for users who meet Discord’s minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. To make a request, contact us below and include the relevant user ID or server ID so we can find the data.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the latest version. Significant changes may also be announced in our support server.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: antispamdevs@gmail.com.
